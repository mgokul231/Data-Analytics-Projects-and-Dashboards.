# Domino's Pizza Orders Dashboard

## Overview
A 3-page interactive Power BI dashboard analyzing Domino's pizza order data for 2024 — sales performance, category-wise breakdown, and time-based ordering patterns. Data was cleaned and prepped in Excel before being modeled and visualized in Power BI.

## Objective
Track how pizza sales are performing against targets, which categories and pizzas sell best, and when customers are actually ordering — to support decisions on menu focus, staffing, and promotions.

## Tools Used
- **Excel** — data cleaning and preparation
- **Power BI** — data modeling, DAX, Power Query, dashboard design

## Dataset Summary
- Order period: 1 Jan 2024 – 31 Dec 2024
- Total sales: 25M, total quantity sold: 50K
- 4 pizza categories: Classic, Supreme, Chicken, Veggie
- Top 5 pizzas by sales: Thai Chicken, Barbecue Chicken, California Chicken, Classic Deluxe, Spicy Italian
- 3 daily time slots: Morning, Afternoon, Evening

## Pages in the Report

### 1. Sales Overview
Headline numbers — total sales, total quantity sold, sales growth %, % of target achieved, and monthly sales. Includes top-selling pizzas by name, category-wise revenue split, a sales-vs-target gauge, monthly sales trend, and monthly sales by category. Filterable by order date.

### 2. Category Performance
A deeper look at the 4 pizza categories — total category sold, and sales for Veggie, Chicken, Supreme, and Classic individually. Includes total orders by category, monthly sales by category, a sales performance/growth tracker, and daily order count by pizza category (by day of week).

### 3. Time Analysis
Ordering-pattern view — average ordering time, monthly/quarterly/previous month/weekly sales. Includes monthly sales by pizza name, quantity sold by time slot (morning/afternoon/evening), quarterly sales by category, daily quantity trend (with increase/decrease indicators), and total orders by time slot.

## Key Skills Demonstrated
- Data cleaning and preparation in Excel
- DAX measures for growth %, target achievement, and time-intelligence (monthly/quarterly trends)
- Category and product-level performance comparison
- Time-slot and day-of-week ordering pattern analysis
- KPI card, gauge, and trend visualization design
- Interactive date-range filtering across a multi-page report
