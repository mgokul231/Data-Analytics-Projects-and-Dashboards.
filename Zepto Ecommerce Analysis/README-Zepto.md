# Zepto Dashboard (DSB)

## Overview
A Power BI dashboard analyzing sales and inventory data for Zepto, the quick-commerce grocery delivery platform. Built to understand product-level sales, stock, and category performance.

## Objective
Show which products and categories are moving fastest, and where stock or pricing issues might be hurting sales.

## Tools Used
- Power BI (data modeling, DAX, Power Query)
- Excel (data prep)

## What the Dashboard Covers
- Total sales and revenue by product category
- Stock availability vs sales performance
- Discount and pricing analysis
- Category-wise and product-wise comparison
- Interactive filters for category and stock status

## Key Skills Demonstrated
- Retail/quick-commerce data analysis
- DAX measures for revenue and stock tracking
- Category-level comparison visuals
- Dashboard design for fast-moving consumer goods data

`ZeptoDSB.pbix` — open in Power BI Desktop to explore the full interactive report.
