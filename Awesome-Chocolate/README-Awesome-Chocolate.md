# Awesome Chocolate Sales Dashboard

## Overview
A Power BI dashboard analyzing sales data for a chocolate company across products, regions, and sales channels. Built on the popular "Awesome Chocolates" practice dataset, commonly used to demonstrate end-to-end Power BI skills.

## Objective
Show which products, countries, and sales people are driving revenue and profit — and where performance is falling short of targets.

## Tools Used
- Power BI (data modeling, DAX, Power Query)
- Excel (data prep)

## What the Dashboard Covers
- Total sales, revenue, and cost of sales overview
- Sales performance by country/region and by product
- Sales person / team performance comparison
- Monthly and yearly sales trends
- Boxes shipped vs. revenue analysis
- Interactive slicers for geography, product, and time period

## Key Skills Demonstrated
- Data modeling with multiple related tables (star schema)
- DAX measures for revenue, cost, and profit tracking
- Geographic (map) visualizations
- Clean, business-style dashboard design
