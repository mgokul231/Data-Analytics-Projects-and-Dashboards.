# Superstore Sales Analysis

## Overview
A Power BI dashboard built on the well-known Superstore retail dataset — a go-to dataset for practicing sales, profit, and discount analysis across products, regions, and customer segments.

## Objective
Identify which products, regions, and customer segments drive profit — and which ones are losing money despite high sales, often due to heavy discounting.

## Tools Used
- Power BI (data modeling, DAX, Power Query)
- Excel (data prep)

## What the Dashboard Covers
- Total sales, profit, and profit margin overview
- Sales and profit by region, state, and city
- Category and sub-category performance
- Discount vs profit relationship
- Customer segment analysis (Consumer, Corporate, Home Office)
- Interactive filters for region, category, and time period

## Key Skills Demonstrated
- Profitability analysis (sales vs. discount vs. margin)
- DAX measures for profit margin and YoY growth
- Geographic and category-level breakdowns
- Classic retail analytics storytelling
