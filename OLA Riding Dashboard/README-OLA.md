# OLA Report

## Overview
A Power BI dashboard analyzing ride-hailing data from OLA — bookings, cancellations, revenue, and driver/vehicle performance. Built to show how the ride business is performing day to day.

## Objective
Give a clear picture of ride volume, cancellation patterns, and revenue trends to help spot where the business is losing rides or money.

## Tools Used
- Power BI (data modeling, DAX, Power Query)
- Excel (data prep)

## What the Dashboard Covers
- Total bookings, completed rides, and cancellation rate
- Revenue trends by day, vehicle type, or payment method
- Cancellation reasons (by customer vs by driver)
- Ride distance and rating analysis
- Interactive filters by date, vehicle type, and status

## Key Skills Demonstrated
- Transactional/ride-data analysis
- DAX measures for cancellation rate and revenue tracking
- Trend and comparison visualizations
- Dashboard design for operations-focused reporting
