# Amazon Sales & Performance Reports

## Overview
A Power BI dashboard analyzing e-commerce sales data in the style of Amazon's marketplace — orders, revenue, product categories, and customer behavior. Built to show a full picture of how sales are performing across products and time.

## Objective
Help a business understand what's selling, what's not, and where revenue is actually coming from — down to category, region, and order status.

## Tools Used
- Power BI (data modeling, DAX, Power Query)
- Excel (data prep)

## What the Dashboard Covers
- Total revenue, orders, and units sold, with trend over time
- Sales breakdown by product category and sub-category
- Order status tracking (delivered, cancelled, returned, pending)
- Top-performing and underperforming products
- Regional/geographic sales distribution
- Interactive filters for date range, category, and region

## Key Skills Demonstrated
- Sales KPI design (revenue, AOV, order volume)
- Data cleaning of messy transactional data
- DAX for time-intelligence (MoM, YoY comparisons)
- Building dashboards for e-commerce performance tracking
